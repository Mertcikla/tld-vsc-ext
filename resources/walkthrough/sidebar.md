## Diagrams and Element Inventory

Click the **tlDiagram** icon in the activity bar to access two native panels:

- **Diagrams** — browse, open, rename, and delete diagrams
- **Element Inventory** — pick reusable elements and drop them into any open diagram

[Show Diagrams](command:tldiagram.focusDiagramsView) [Show Element Inventory](command:tldiagram.focusElementInventoryView)
